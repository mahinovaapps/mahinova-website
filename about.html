About mahinova

We turn good ideas into apps people love to use.

mahinova is a software and mobile app studio based in Palashipara, Nadia, West Bengal, working with businesses across India.

Our story

Why mahinova exists.

mahinova started with one simple goal: help small businesses and new founders get the same quality software that big companies have, without the confusion or the big-city price tag.

Many good ideas never get built because the process feels too technical or too risky. We take care of the technology, explain every step in plain language and stay with you after launch.
