Questions

Questions people ask about websites.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The design, the content, the domain and the hosting account are in your name. We never resell them at a markup.

Will my website show on Google?

We set up the basics: page titles, a sitemap, Google Search Console and your Google Business profile. Ranking takes a few weeks, and we tell you what to post to rank higher in your town.

Can I change the text and photos myself?

With the Business Site and Online Store you get a simple panel to edit your own content. For a Starter Site we make the changes for you, quickly.

How do I pay?

40% to start, 40% after the demo and 20% at launch, by UPI or bank transfer. The price you agree is the price you pay.

Can the website be in Bangla?

Yes. We can write and design it in Bangla, Hindi or English, or in two languages together.
