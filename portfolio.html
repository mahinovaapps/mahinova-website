Featured project

QR menu and billing for restaurants.

A web app that runs on any phone, with no app to download. Made by mahinova for restaurants and cafes.

The problem

Small restaurants juggle paper menus, shouted orders and hand-written bills. Orders get missed and the day's sales are a guess.

What we built

A QR menu for guests, a kitchen screen, GST billing and sales reports, all in one system with staff roles.

How it is made

A Next.js web app that installs on a phone like an app. Works in English and Bangla.
