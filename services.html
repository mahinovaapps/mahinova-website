FAQ

Questions people often ask.

How much does an app or website cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short call we send a fixed quote in rupees, so you know the full cost before any work starts. Small websites cost far less than a full app, and we can suggest a smaller first version to fit your budget.

How long does it take?

A simple website usually takes one to three weeks. Most apps take one to three months, depending on the features. You will get a clear timeline with your quote.

Will I own the app and the code?

Yes. The code, designs, app store listings and accounts belong to you.

Can you build for both Android and iPhone?

Yes. We usually build both from one shared codebase, which saves time and money.

Do you help after the launch?

Yes. We fix launch issues quickly and offer monthly care plans for updates and new features.

How do payments work?

We usually split payment into stages: a part to start, a part after design approval and the rest at launch.
